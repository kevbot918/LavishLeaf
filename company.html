About Us

Lavish Leaf Inc is an Oklahoma company built around one idea: a good life should be good for the environment and its inhabitants too. Today that means sustainable farming, composting and recycling, community involvement, and education. Tomorrow it may mean renewable energy, eco-friendly homes, organic restaurants, and more!

Lavish Leaf is currently one person. Everything here is being built to grow past that: to earn its own way, to benefit the people, and to give communities more places to grow, play and gather.

About the Founder

Kevin Wright, Founder

Kevin was born and raised in southeastern Oklahoma. He earned a degree in Business Administration at Missouri Southern State University, where he was an All-American in track and field.

Since 2016 he has been a teacher and a coach. He has taught everything from high school astronomy, science and computers to elementary math, science, reading and social studies, and he has coached track and field, cross country, soccer, tennis and archery.

Lavish Leaf comes from the idea of living a more wholesome life by connecting with nature and community. It aims to help communities live a more eco-friendly, sustainable and organic life, for the betterment of our planet and our people.

How We Are Organized

This is the structure we are growing into. Most of these seats are empty today, and each fills as the part of the company it serves can support it.

Shareholders

Board of Directors and Officers

Elected by the shareholders. Every director is also an officer, and all are equal: together they run the administration and the executive work, set direction, and keep the company true to its mission.

A division may be led by one of the officers or by a manager of its own, and the roles below report to whoever leads their division.

  • Lavish Leaf Farms: every farm location has its own manager, who runs that farm's growing and the compost and recycling pick-up routes around it.
  • Rec Sports: league coordinators run each league's season, from registrations and schedules to referees and tournaments.
  • Lavish Leaf Gaming: a studio lead runs the games, Unity assets and servers as the studio grows.
  • Symphonymph: the officers look after the app directly.
  • Online Store: an officer runs the suppliers, orders and shipping until the store is ready for a manager of its own.

Interested in serving on our board, or in a future partnership with Lavish Leaf? Email careers@lavishleaf.org and tell us about yourself. We are not offering shares or any other investment through this website.

Future Roles

We are not hiring yet. These are the jobs we plan to fill, and every one of them will be paid as its division starts earning. If one fits you, email careers@lavishleaf.org and we will reach out first when it opens.

Farm Hand

Farms

Description: Assist with day to day operations of the farm. Watering, planting, harvesting, and more.

Responsibilities may include

  • Water plants when needed and check and monitor soil and plants
  • Harvest produce and wash and package produce
  • Fill germination trays, make soil blocks, and sow seeds
  • Transplant seedlings, repot plants, and clean equipment
  • De-weed garden beds and monitor garden for diseases and pests
  • Mix and make compost teas and fertilizer and spread onto garden beds

Pick-Up Driver

Farms

Description: Pick up compost and recyclables from customers, and deliver produce and plants.

Responsibilities may include

  • Pick up compost buckets and bring them to Lavish Leaf Farms
  • Empty customers' recycling bins and take them to the recycling centers
  • Sort recycling materials
  • Teach customers what can be composted and recycled
  • Train other pick-up drivers

Social Media Coordinator

All divisions

Description: Promote Lavish Leaf and interact with our customers on Lavish Leaf's social media accounts.

Responsibilities may include

  • Create content, posts, and ads to promote our products and services
  • Reply and respond to comments and direct messages
  • Support, guide, interact, and connect our customers through social media
  • Collaborate and assist management with promotional goods and services
  • Regularly take pictures and videos of the farm, the league and the studio

Game Developer

Gaming

Description: Build Lavish Leaf's games and the worlds and servers they run on.

Responsibilities may include

  • Game development and Unity asset creation
  • Server administration for our game servers
  • Gaming content creation and streaming
  • Testing, bug reports and player support

Rec Sports League Coordinator

Rec Sports

Description: Run our adult rec leagues and tournaments from sign-up to the final whistle.

Responsibilities may include

  • Build schedules and keep standings and results up to date
  • Handle team and player registrations and waivers
  • Book fields, line up referees and set up equipment on game day
  • Answer captains' and players' questions
  • Find sponsors and plan tournaments and events

Want to help Lavish Leaf grow before then? Donations support the work directly and are not tax-deductible.
