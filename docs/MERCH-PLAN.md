# Own merch for less than Printful: the plan (researched 2026-10-14)

Prices are estimates from vendor pages and search snippets; confirm each in
the vendor's cart before ordering. Margin = (price - unit cost - PayPal
3.49% + $0.49) / price. Costs are S to XL on black.

## Cost per item
| | Printful now | Own batch (blank + DTF transfer + mailer) |
|---|---|---|
| Organic tee | $14.25 to $20.50 | about $11.50 (Allmade AL2100 or econscious EC1000, 11x11 in DTF about $3.50) |
| Women's organic tee | $15.91 to $17.91 | about $11.75 (econscious EC3000) |
| Hoodie | $27.84 to $33.84 | about $19 (same Cotton Heritage M2580 blank, DTF $4) |
| Trucker cap | $18.25 | about $13 (Richardson 112 blank $9.26 + heat-seal patch $3.11 at 50+) |
| 3x3 sticker | $2.34 | $0.73 at 100, $0.47 at 200 (Sticker Mule, free shipping) |

Cheaper print on demand does not help: Printify Premium ($299 a year),
Gelato and Gooten are only cheaper on conventional cotton; on organic blanks
Printify's econscious tee ($15) costs more than Printful's. Fulfillment
warehouses do not fit this volume (Printful ended warehousing 2026-03-01;
Simpl $750 a month minimum).

## Phase 1 (now, about an hour)
* Keep Printful for apparel (no stock, no packing).
* Stickers move to Sticker Mule: 100 Lavish Leaf + 100 Symphonymph, about $146;
  mail in a stamped envelope; sticker price can drop to $3.50.
* Order samples: Ninja Transfers DTF sample pack, one AL2100 tee, one M2580 hoodie.

## Phase 2 (when apparel sells about 15 a month for two months)
* Blanks from a no-minimum reseller (Blankstyle, Ninja Blanks) or an S&S /
  SanMar account; DTF transfers from Ninja Transfers or Stahls; caps with
  heat-seal patches.
* Equipment: 15x15 heat press $150 to $175, hat press $80 to $130.
* Starter batch: tees 36 (18 per design: S 4, M 9, L 11, XL 7, 2XL 5),
  hoodies 12 (S 1, M 3, L 4, XL 2, 2XL 2), caps 24, 200 more stickers.
  About $1,370 with presses and mailers. Break-even at about 51% sold
  (today's prices) or 61% (lower prices). 3XL to 5XL stay on Printful.
* Shipping from Stigler by Pirate Ship (USPS Ground Advantage): tee about
  $4.30 to $5.70, hoodie about $9 to $10; poly mailers $0.09 to $0.28.
* About 10 to 15 minutes per order, plus a monthly pressing session.
* The store would need a "shipFrom: own" rule at our postage cost and a
  stock count per size (the stock check can read it).

## Phase 3 (optional)
Once one design sells 48+, a local Tulsa/OKC screen-print quote for the
one-color Lavish Leaf oval ($10 to $14 a shirt at 24 to 47 plus $20 to $50 a color).
