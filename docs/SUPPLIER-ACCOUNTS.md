## Before any account: the two papers every wholesaler asks for

1. **EIN** (free, irs.gov, "Apply for an EIN online", about 10 minutes). Lavish
   Leaf Inc is a corporation, so it needs its own number; keep the confirmation
   letter (CP 575) as a PDF.
2. **Oklahoma sales tax permit and resale certificate.** The permit is filed;
   the resale certificate is the Oklahoma Tax Commission's resale exemption
   form (find the current one on oklahoma.gov/tax, OkTAP), filled in once per
   supplier with the permit number. Keep a signed copy ready to upload.

Have ready for every application: the legal name (Lavish Leaf Inc), the EIN,
the permit number, the website (lavishleaf.org/store.html), a business email
(support@lavishleaf.org), and one sentence on the store: *"An online store in
Eastern Oklahoma for organic garden supplies and low-waste household goods."*
No street address goes on the website; the applications themselves do need
the mailing address.
