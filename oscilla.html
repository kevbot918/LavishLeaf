Oscilla

Oscilla Free Audio

Podcasts, audiobooks, internet radio, lectures and independent music — drawn from the world's public-domain and openly-licensed archives. Free to listen. No ads. No account. No subscription.

 Android first, in active development. Sign up below to hear when it launches.

Why We Built It

Listening got expensive. The paid apps cost more every year, and the free ones make you the product — full-screen ads between tracks, countdowns you have to sit through, buttons you have to hunt for to close. We wanted an app that simply plays audio, and stops there.

It turns out there is an enormous amount of audio that is already free and legal to listen to. Libraries, universities, archives, broadcasters and independent artists have been publishing openly for decades. The problem was never supply. It was that nobody had put it all in one place with a decent player in front of it.

That is Oscilla: millions of recordings from the world's open catalogs, in one app, with nothing between you and the play button.

What's Inside

Audiobooks & Poetry

Tens of thousands of complete audiobooks read by volunteers, plus poetry and spoken-word collections — the classics, in full, free because the works themselves are in the public domain. Resume where you left off, download for offline, listen at your own pace.

Podcasts

An open, independent podcast directory — not one company's walled catalog. Shows are ranked by what people are actually listening to this week, and every episode streams from the publisher's own feed, exactly as any podcast app does.

Internet Radio

Live stations from nearly every country on earth, from community broadcasters to national services. Filter by language so you hear the ones you can actually understand — or leave it open and go exploring.

Independent Music

Artist-distributed tracks, Creative Commons releases from netlabels, and a vast archive of live concert recordings from bands who explicitly allow taping and sharing. Hundreds of thousands of shows, free and legal.

Lectures & Talks

University lecture recordings, conference talks and educational series — the kind of material that used to sit behind a campus login. Sorted alongside everything else, so learning is one tap from listening.

Radio Drama & More

Classic old-time radio serials, news, meditation and sleep audio, religion and spirituality, and material in dozens of languages. New categories are added as we verify new archives.

Four Promises

No ads. Not any kind. Not banners, not interstitials, not audio spots, not "watch this to keep listening." We looked hard at ad-supported models and concluded that at our size the revenue would be too small to pay artists anything meaningful — and it would have disqualified a large part of our catalog, because many open licences forbid commercial use. Staying ad-free is what keeps the library as big as it is.

No account required. Open the app and press play. Ever. If we later add optional sign-in for syncing your playlists between devices, it will be exactly that — optional, and never a gate in front of listening.

We never host anyone's audio. Oscilla points your phone at each rightsholder's own server and streams from there, the same way a web browser or a podcast app does. We are a player, not a middleman, and we never re-host, cache or re-publish someone else's recording.

Credit where it is owed. Many open licences require the creator to be named wherever their work plays. Oscilla shows the licence and the credit on the Now Playing screen, every time. That is a legal obligation, not a courtesy, and we treat it as one.

Artists: Share Your Work

If you make audio — music, a podcast, spoken word, field recordings, anything — and you would like it available in Oscilla, send us a link. We will review it and, if it fits, index it so listeners can find it.

Send a link, not a file. Oscilla never hosts audio, so your work stays exactly where you already put it — your own site, your podcast feed, Bandcamp, ccMixter, Audius, or anywhere else that serves the audio publicly. You keep ownership and you keep control: take it down at your end and it disappears from the app.

No host of your own? The Internet Archive will store your audio free and permanently, for anyone, and Oscilla already reads it. Upload there, then paste the link below.

To be clear about money: Oscilla pays nothing, because Oscilla earns nothing. There are no ads to split and no subscription revenue. What we can do is give your work an audience and put a link to your own donation or purchase page beside it, so anything a listener chooses to give goes to you in full.

A podcast RSS feed, an Internet Archive item, a Bandcamp or ccMixter page, or a direct link to the files.

Not sure? Say so and we will help you pick. It does not have to be Creative Commons — we only need your permission to link to it.

Ko-fi, Patreon, PayPal, Bandcamp — wherever listeners can support you directly. 100% goes to you; we never take a cut.

Keeping It Free

Oscilla has no ads and no subscription, so it runs on donations and on the goodwill of the archives that make it possible. The catalogs behind the app — public libraries, universities, volunteer-run directories and nonprofit archives — are the real work. If you have something to give, give it to them first; we will always tell you who they are.

Down the road there may be one optional purchase: a single one-time payment to sync your playlists and place across devices. Buy it once, keep it. Never a subscription, and never required to listen.
