What We Grow

Our farm produces a variety of vegetables, seedlings, and free-ranged chicken and duck eggs, grown and raised using sustainable, chemical-free methods. We primarily focus on growing tomatoes, okra, cucumbers, squash, zucchini, corn, green beans, peppers, and sunflowers.

We also cultivate seedlings and transplants for home gardeners and community gardens. All produce is grown right here in Eastern Oklahoma, tended with care and love for the land.

Compost Pick-Up Service

$20.00 every month

HOW IT WORKS: We give you a 5 gallon bucket and swap it for a clean one every 1 to 2 weeks, or sooner and more often, depending on how quickly you fill it.

Reduce your trash and help us enrich the soil naturally. When food scraps go to a landfill, the methane they release is about 80x worse for the climate than CO2. Here, they become living soil for our farm and our customers.

Compost instead of trash:

  • Fruit and vegetable scraps, peels and cores
  • Coffee grounds and paper coffee filters
  • Tea leaves and plastic-free tea bags
  • Eggshells
  • Bread, pasta, rice, grains and cereal
  • Nut shells and corn cobs
  • Houseplant trimmings and wilted flowers
  • Lawn clippings, leaves, small twigs and mulch
  • Untreated wood chips and sawdust
  • Ink-free paper, shredded paper, paper towels and napkins
  • Plain cardboard and paper egg cartons
  • Hair and pet fur
  • Certified compostable bags and plates

Please leave out: meat, fish, bones, dairy, grease and oils, pet waste, glossy or coated paper, and anything plastic.

  • Pick-up days are your choice: whichever day of the week you prefer.
  • Pick-up is local only. Get in touch and we will tell you whether you are in range.

Recycling Pick-Up Service

$20.00 every month

HOW IT WORKS: You provide your own recycling bin. On your pick-up day we empty it, and eventually take it to local recycling centers or repurpose it, so it gets recycled instead of buried.

What we take:

  • Cardboard, flattened
  • Mixed paper: mail, newspaper, magazines, office paper
  • Aluminum and tin cans
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Plastic #1 and #2 (look for the number in the triangle)
  • Anything else the McAlester and Checotah recycling centers accept

Please: rinse containers so they are free of food, and leave out plastic bags, Styrofoam, plastics #3 to #7, electronics and anything greasy or wet. The recycling centers turn away dirty loads.

  • You provide the bin; we empty it.
  • Pick-up days are your choice: whichever day of the week you prefer.
  • Pick-up is local only. Get in touch and we will tell you whether you are in range.
